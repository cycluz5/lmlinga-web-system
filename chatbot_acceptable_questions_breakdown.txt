LMLinga Health Chatbot — Acceptable Questions Breakdown
=========================================================
Languages: English (en) | Tagalog (tl) | Bikol–Iriga (bcl)
Generated from conversation services + RagService intent detectors.
Source files:
  - app/Services/EnglishConversationService.php
  - app/Services/TagalogConversationService.php
  - app/Services/BikolConversationService.php
  - app/Services/RagService.php
  - storage/app/health_docs/{en|tl|bcl}/...

HOW THE CHATBOT FLOW WORKS
--------------------------
1. User picks a language in the UI (English / Tagalog / Bikol – Iriga).
2. Message is checked against EXACT canned chit-chat phrases (greeting, thanks,
   goodbye, identity, capabilities, etc.).
   - Exact match (ignoring ?!.,;: and extra spaces) → fixed reply, done.
   - Greeting + health question in one message (e.g. "Hi, what is dengue?")
     → greeting stripped, rest goes to health Q&A.
3. Health questions go through RAG:
   - Keyword match on health docs (then embedding fallback).
   - Intent detected from cue words (symptoms, causes, prevention, etc.).
   - Answer built from matching document sections.
4. If no matching health material is found, the bot says it does not have
   enough information (language-specific fallback message).

IMPORTANT
---------
- Chit-chat: ONLY the exact phrases listed below are accepted as canned replies.
- Health Q&A: phrasing can vary, but questions should use the cue words and
  topics listed below so intent + document retrieval succeed.
- Answers are limited to indexed health materials (not open-ended medical advice).

###############################################################################
# PART B — HEALTH QUESTION PATTERNS (by language / intent)
###############################################################################
Replace {topic} with any topic in Part C (e.g. dengue, hypertension, malaria).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGLISH health intents (detectEnglishSupportIntent)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cue words → intent

  symptom / symptoms / sign / signs
      → symptoms
      Examples:
        What are the symptoms of {topic}?
        What are the signs of {topic}?

  cause / causes / why ... occur|happen|develop / risk factor(s)
      → causes
      Examples:
        What causes {topic}?
        Why does {topic} happen?
        What are the risk factors for {topic}?

  prevent / prevention / preventing / prevents
      → prevention
      Examples:
        How can {topic} be prevented?
        How to prevent {topic}?

  treat / treatment / treating / treated
      → treatment
      Examples:
        What is the treatment for {topic}?
        How is {topic} treated?

  benefit / benefits
      → benefits
      Examples:
        What are the benefits of {topic}?

  what is / what are  (at start of question)
      → definition
      Examples:
        What is {topic}?
        What are {topic}?

  (no cue match)
      → general

Combined with greeting (accepted):
  Hi, what is hypertension?
  Hello, what are the symptoms of dengue?

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAGALOG health intents (detectTagalogSupportIntent)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cue words → intent

  sintomas / senyales / palatandaan
      → symptoms
      Examples:
        Ano ang mga sintomas ng {topic}?
        Ano ang mga senyales ng {topic}?

  maiwasan / maiiwasan / pag-iwas / iwasan
      → prevention
      Examples:
        Paano maiiwasan ang {topic}?
        Ano ang pag-iwas sa {topic}?

  paggamot / gamot / ginagamot / gamutin / lunas / treatment
      → treatment
      Examples:
        Ano ang paggamot sa {topic}?
        Ano ang gamot para sa {topic}?

  sanhi / dahilan / rason / bakit / nagkakaroon
      → causes
      Examples:
        Ano ang sanhi ng {topic}?
        Bakit nagkakaroon ng {topic}?

  benepisyo / benipisyo
      → benefits
      Examples:
        Ano ang benepisyo ng {topic}?

  dapat / gawin / gagawin
      → action
      Examples:
        Ano ang dapat gawin kung may {topic}?

  operasyon
      → unsupported (bot will not invent an answer)

  ano ang
      → definition
      Examples:
        Ano ang {topic}?

  (no cue match)
      → general

Combined with greeting (accepted):
  Kumusta, ano ang dengue?
  Hi, ano ang mga sintomas ng hypertension?

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIKOL–IRIGA health intents (detectBikolIntent)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cue words → intent (order matters; richer than EN/TL)

  magpakonsulta / dadarahon / ospital / health center /
  failed test / positive + resulta|screening|test /
  kombolsyon / alangkaw + kalintura / malala + sinyalis
      → warning
      Examples:
        Kailangan ko magpakonsulta kin agko sintomas ka dengue?
        Uno a gigibuhon kin positive a screening result?

  sintomas / senyales / sinyalis / palatandaan / katangian /
  pag-uugali / sensitibo / tunog / sulo / liwanag
      → symptoms
      Examples:
        Uno an mga sintomas ka {topic}?
        Uno a mga senyales ka {topic}?

  maisihan / pagkaisi / early detection / pagsukol / monitor /
  screening (unless plain "Uno a ... screening?")
      → diagnosis
      Examples:
        Pauno maisihan an {topic}?
        Uno a screening para sa {topic}?

  ngaya / benepisyo / ngata + agko (not nagkaka-agko)
      → benefits
      Examples:
        Uno a benepisyo ka {topic}?
        Ngata agko {topic}?

  ngata / dahilan / rason / peligro / risk / namamana / genetic
      → causes
      Examples:
        Ngata nagkaka-agko {topic}?
        Uno a mga dahilan o risk factor ka {topic}?
        Pauno nagkaka-agko dengue?

  maiwasan / likay / pag-iwas / makalikay
  OR gibuhon / bulong / gamot / ataman / kontrol / suporta
      → management  (prevention + treatment share this bucket in Bikol)
      Examples:
        Pauno maiwasan an {topic}?
        Uno a gigibuhon kin agko {topic}?
        Uno a gamot o bulong sa {topic}?

  epekto / mangyayari / komplikasyon
      → effects
      Examples:
        Uno a epekto ka {topic}?

  kuno / edad / bulan / taon / milestone / iskedyul /
  numeric age (e.g. 9 na bulan)
      → schedule
      Examples:
        Uno a bakuna sa 9 na bulan?
        Kuno a milestone ka baby?

  uno  (when no more-specific cue matched)
      → definition
      Examples:
        Uno an dengue?
        Uno ana hypertension?
        Uno a {topic}?

  (no cue match)
      → general

Official few-shot style examples from BikolPrompts:
  Uno an dengue?
  Uno an mga sintomas ka dengue?
  Pauno nagkaka-agko dengue?
  Pauno maiwasan an dengue?
  Uno ana hypertension?
  Uno a mga pwedeng dahilan o risk factor ka hypertension?

Combined with greeting (accepted):
  Kumusta, uno an dengue?
  Hi, pauno maiwasan an malaria?

###############################################################################
# PART E — WHAT IS NOT ACCEPTABLE / LIKELY TO FAIL
###############################################################################
- Random chit-chat outside the exact canned phrase lists above
- Topics not in the health_docs corpus (e.g. unrelated diseases, personal diagnosis)
- Tagalog "operasyon" questions (explicitly unsupported)
- Questions that invent clinical advice beyond the materials
- Very vague questions with no topic keyword and no prior conversation context

Fallback replies when no info is found:
  EN:  I don't have enough information on that yet. Could you try rephrasing?
  TL:  Wala akong sapat na impormasyon tungkol dito. Pakisubukang muli.
  BCL: Uda ako sapat na impormasyon manungod sa unga mo. Uliton ulit.
