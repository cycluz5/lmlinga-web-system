# Bikol-Iriga Bible lookup guide (New Testament)

Use with `vocabulary_guide.csv`. Find the verse in the printed Bikol-Iriga NT,
spot the word, and write it in the `bikol_iriga` column.

- Verse numbers can differ by one between translations — read the verses around it.
- Write the **base word** (e.g. the word for "fever"), not the whole phrase.
- Bible wording can be formal or old. If people say it differently today, put the
  everyday word in `bikol_iriga` and the Bible word in `bikol_variants`.
- Do **not** copy whole verses into the project — the translation is copyrighted.
- Tick the box when done.

## Not in the Bible — ask residents / health workers

Vaccine, dengue, mosquito, hypertension / high blood, diabetes, cancer,
tuberculosis, asthma, family planning, contraceptives, vitamins, nutrition,
check-up, health center, hospital, cough, colds, headache, dizziness, rash.
Many of these are borrowed from English/Tagalog — write the form residents use.
