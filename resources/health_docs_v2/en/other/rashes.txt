A skin rash is a change in the appearance of the skin that may cause redness, itching, or irritation. It can be caused by allergies, infections, insect bites, or skin irritation.

Signs and Symptoms
- Redness or discoloration of the skin.
- Itching or irritation.
- Small bumps or blisters.
- Swelling of the affected area.
- Dry or peeling skin.

Prevention
- Keep the skin clean and dry.
- Avoid products that cause allergies or irritation.
- Use mild soap and skin-friendly products.
- Avoid scratching the affected area.
- Wear clean and comfortable clothes.
- Avoid sharing personal items such as towels and clothing.
