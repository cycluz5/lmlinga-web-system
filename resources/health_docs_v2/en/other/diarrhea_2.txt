Diarrhea is the frequent passing of soft or watery stool, usually three or more times within one day.
It is often caused by infection, contaminated food or water, and an unclean environment.

Signs and Symptoms
- Frequent bowel movements.
- Soft or watery stool.
- Stomach pain or cramps.
- Nausea and vomiting.
- Fever.
- Dizziness and weakness.
- Dry mouth and extreme thirst (signs of dehydration).

Prevention
- Always wash your hands with soap and water.
- Drink clean and safe water.
- Cook food thoroughly.
- Wash fruits and vegetables before eating them.
- Avoid spoiled or contaminated food.
- Keep your body and surroundings clean.
