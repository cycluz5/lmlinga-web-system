Ovarian cancer is a type of cancer that begins in the ovaries, the female reproductive organs responsible for producing eggs and hormones such as estrogen and progesterone.

Symptoms
- Abdominal Bloating or swelling
- Pelvic or abdominal pain
- Frequent urination or an urgent need to urinate
- Changes in bowel habits
- Unexplained weight loss
- Fatigue
- Back pain

Prevention
- Maintain a healthy lifestyle and balanced diet.
- Exercise regularly and maintain a healthy weight.
- Discuss family history of ovarian or breast cancer with a healthcare professional.
- Consider genetic counseling and testing if there is a strong family history of ovarian or breast cancer.
- Discuss risk-reducing options with a healthcare professional if at high risk.
