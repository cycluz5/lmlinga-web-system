Obesity is a condition where a person has too much body fat, which may affect their health. It is commonly caused by unhealthy eating habits, lack of physical activity, and other factors such as genetics.
Signs and Symptoms:
- Excessive body weight.
- Increased body fat, especially around the waist.
- Easily gets tired.
- Shortness of breath during physical activities.
- Difficulty moving or performing daily activities.
- Joint and back pain.
Prevention:
Maintain a healthy weight by eating nutritious and balanced meals, exercising regularly, drinking enough water, avoiding excessive consumption of sugary and fatty foods, and getting enough sleep.
