A UTI is an infection in any part of the urinary system, including the kidneys, bladder, and urethra. It is commonly caused by bacteria entering the urinary tract.

Signs and Symptoms
- Pain or burning sensation when urinating.
- Frequent urge to urinate.
- Passing only small amounts of urine.
- Cloudy or foul-smelling urine.
- Lower abdominal pain.
- Sometimes fever and back pain, especially if the infection reaches the kidneys.

Prevention
- Drink plenty of clean water.
- Do not hold urine for too long.
- Maintain proper genital hygiene.
- Wipe from front to back after using the toilet.
- Urinate after sexual intercourse.
- Avoid using irritating feminine hygiene products.
