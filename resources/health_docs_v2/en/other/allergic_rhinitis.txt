Allergic rhinitis is an allergic reaction that occurs when the immune system reacts to allergens such as dust, pollen, mold, or pet dander. It causes inflammation of the nose and can lead to cold-like symptoms.

Signs and Symptoms
- Frequent sneezing.
- Runny or stuffy nose.
- Itchy nose, throat, or eyes.
- Watery eyes.
- Postnasal drip (mucus dripping down the throat).
- Nasal congestion.

Prevention
- Avoid exposure to dust, pollen, and other allergens.
- Keep the house clean and dust-free.
- Wash bed sheets and pillowcases regularly.
- Avoid cigarette smoke and strong fragrances.
- Wear a mask when cleaning or exposed to dust.
- Keep windows closed when pollen or dust levels are high.
