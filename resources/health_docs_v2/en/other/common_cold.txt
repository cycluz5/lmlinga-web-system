The common cold is an infection of the upper respiratory tract, usually caused by a virus, that affects the nose and throat.

Symptoms
- Sneezing
- Stuffy or runny nose
- Sore throat
- Cough
- Mild headache
- Fatigue
- Sometimes fever

Prevention
- Wash hands regularly with soap and water.
- Avoid touching the eyes, nose, and mouth with unclean hands
- Cover the mouth and nose when coughing or sneezing.
- Avoid close contact with people who have a cold.
- Eat nutritious food and get enough sleep.
- Keep the surroundings clean and well-ventilated.
