Foodborne Illness
A foodborne disease is an illness caused by eating or drinking contaminated food or water containing harmful microorganisms, such as bacteria, viruses, parasites, or toxins.
Symptoms:
- Diarrhea
- Nausea and vomiting
- Abdominal pain
- Fever
- Headache
- Weakness
- Dehydration
Prevention:
- Wash your hands before preparing and eating food.
- Wash fruits and vegetables thoroughly.
- Cook meat, fish, and other foods properly.
- Avoid eating raw or undercooked foods.
- Use clean water for cooking and drinking.
- Separate raw foods from cooked foods to prevent contamination.
- Store food properly in the refrigerator to prevent bacterial growth.
- Keep food preparation areas and utensils clean.
- Avoid eating spoiled or expired food.
