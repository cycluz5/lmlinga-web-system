Nausea is the uncomfortable feeling of wanting to vomit, while vomiting is the forceful release of stomach contents through the mouth. These may be caused by infections, food poisoning, motion sickness, or certain medications.

Signs and Symptoms
- Feeling of wanting to vomit.
- Actual vomiting.
- Stomach discomfort or pain.
- Dizziness.
- Excessive sweating.
- Loss of appetite.
- Weakness and dehydration.

Prevention
- Eat clean and properly prepared food.
- Avoid spoiled or contaminated food.
- Drink clean and safe water.
- Eat small and frequent meals.
- Avoid overeating and greasy foods.
- Wash hands before eating and preparing food.
- Get enough rest.
- Avoid strong smells that may trigger nausea.
