Modern Birth Spacing Methods
Condom, for Men
98% effective
Worn by the man over his penis before sexual intercourse.
Effective when worn and removed correctly.
Helps protect against sexually transmitted infections (STIs).
Injectables, for Women
Two Types of Injectables:
Combined Injectable Contraceptive
99.95% effective
Only one injection every month.
Helps make menstrual periods more regular.
Can be stopped anytime when a woman wants to become pregnant again.
Progestin-Only Injectable
99.7% effective
Only one injection every 3 months.
Does not affect breastfeeding or sexual intercourse.
Pills, for Women
Effective.
Does not interfere with sexual intercourse.
Taken by the woman every day at the same time.
Can be stopped anytime when a woman wants to become pregnant again.
Two Types of Pills:
Combined Oral Contraceptives (COCs)
99.7% effective
Prevent ovulation (the release of a mature egg from a woman's ovary).
Contain the hormones estrogen and progestin, which prevent pregnancy.
Not suitable for breastfeeding mothers.
Progestin-Only Pills (POPs)
99.5% effective
Contain progestin, which prevents ovulation.
Suitable for breastfeeding mothers because they do not reduce the flow, amount, or quality of breast milk.
Standard Days Method (SDM)
95% effective
Uses CycleBeads to help a woman or couple identify the woman's fertile days so they can avoid sexual intercourse to prevent pregnancy.
Suitable for women with a menstrual cycle of 26–32 days.
Suitable for couples who are able to abstain from sexual intercourse during the woman's fertile period.
Lactational Amenorrhea Method (LAM)
99.5% effective
Prevents ovulation. 
To be effective, all of the following conditions must be met:
The baby is exclusively breastfed.
The mother's menstrual period has not yet returned.
The baby is less than six months old. If any of these conditions no longer applies, LAM is no longer effective.
Basal Body Temperature (BBT)
99% effective
To determine when she is fertile and may become pregnant through sexual intercourse, a woman takes her temperature before getting out of bed after at least three consecutive hours of sleep.
Suitable for couples who are able to abstain from sexual intercourse during the woman's fertile period.
Billings Ovulation/Cervical Mucus Method
97% effective
The woman observes the type of mucus discharged from her vagina to determine when she is fertile and may become pregnant through sexual intercourse.
Suitable for couples who are able to abstain from sexual intercourse during the woman's fertile period.
Sympto-Thermal Method
98% effective
This method involves observing body temperature together with several signs of fertility, such as vaginal mucus, lower abdominal pain, and other symptoms.
Suitable for couples who are able to abstain from sexual intercourse during the woman's fertile period.
Long-Acting and Permanent Methods
Intrauterine Device (IUD)
99.4% effective
Effective for up to 10 years.
A small, soft plastic device inserted into a woman's uterus.
Does not affect breastfeeding or sexual intercourse.
No-Scalpel Vasectomy (NSV), for Men
99.9% effective
The left and right vas deferens, which carry sperm, are tied and cut.
Does not affect sexual intercourse with one's wife or a man's masculinity.
Bilateral Tubal Ligation (BTL), for Women
99.5% effective
The woman's left and right fallopian tubes are tied and cut.
Does not affect sexual intercourse with one's husband. All of these methods are safe when used correctly.
Benefits of Family Planning
Mother – You can regain your strength more quickly after pregnancy and childbirth; you will be better prepared for every pregnancy; you will have more time for your family—for your children, your husband, and yourself; and you will be able to care for and raise your children better.
Father – Your family will enjoy a better quality of life; you can save money for your children's education and other needs; you will have more time for your family; and you will be able to fulfill your responsibilities more effectively.
Baby and Children – They will receive adequate time for breastfeeding, immunization, proper nutrition, and dental care; and they will be provided with sufficient education, love, care, support, and proper values formation.
Whole Family – Your family's livelihood will be more stable; you will have more time for one another; and you will be better able to protect your health and meet your family's needs.
Frequently Asked Questions About Family Planning
What is family planning?
It is the use of modern, safe, and effective methods that enable couples to achieve their desired number of children and proper spacing between pregnancies. Proper birth spacing means allowing an interval of three to five years between pregnancies.
"Family planning refers to a program which enables couples and individuals to decide freely and responsibly the number and spacing of their children and to have the information and means to do so, and to have access to a full range of safe, affordable, effective, non-abortifacient modern natural and artificial methods of planning pregnancy."
Is family planning a form of abortion?
No. Abortion is the termination of a pregnancy. Family planning, on the other hand, is the prevention of pregnancy. Through family planning, abortion can be avoided because it helps couples prevent unplanned pregnancies.
Are family planning methods harmful to health?
No. All family planning methods are safe and effective when used correctly. Couples can choose the method that best suits their bodies, health, and personal needs.
Do family planning methods increase or decrease sexual desire?
Each person has a different level of sexual desire. In general, family planning methods do not affect a person's sexual desire. By using family planning, couples are relieved of the worry of having an unplanned pregnancy. As a result, they are freer to express the physical aspect of their love for one another.
Are family planning methods expensive?
Most family planning methods are available in pharmacies at affordable prices. Some companies also provide family planning methods that are appropriate for their employees. The costs of tubal ligation, vasectomy, and IUD insertion are covered under the PhilHealth benefit package.
For more information about family planning, consult your doctor, nurse, or midwife at the clinic, health center, or hospital nearest you.
