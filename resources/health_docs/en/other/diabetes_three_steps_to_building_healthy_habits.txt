Diabetes: Three Steps to Building Healthy Habits

Key Points

Developing healthy habits is an important part of living a healthier life, maintaining good health as you age, and managing diabetes or other long-term health conditions. Although changing your lifestyle can be challenging, creating healthy habits is possible with proper planning, commitment, and consistency.

Step 1: Create a Plan
Before starting a new healthy habit, take time to make a clear plan. First, identify your reason for making the change. Ask yourself why you want to improve your health and how your life will benefit once you achieve your goal. Writing down your motivation and placing it somewhere visible can remind you to stay focused whenever you face challenges.

Next, decide on a long-term health goal and choose a specific habit that will help you reach it. For example, if your goal is to improve your diet, you may plan to eat more fruits and vegetables every day.

Make your goal SMART, which means it should be:
Specific – Clearly describe what you want to do.
Measurable – Make it easy to track your progress.
Achievable – Set a goal that is realistic.
Relevant – Make sure it supports your health goals.
Time-bound – Set a target schedule or deadline.

For example, instead of saying, "I will eat more vegetables," choose a specific goal such as, "I will eat a side salad with dinner every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday."

Step 2: Stay Accountable
Keeping track of your progress can help you build and maintain healthy habits. Monitoring your daily actions allows you to see what works well and what may need improvement. It also motivates you to continue working toward your goals.
You can record your progress using a mobile app, food diary, notebook, or spreadsheet. Some people also benefit from support provided by family members, friends, exercise partners, or other people who encourage them to stay committed.

For people with diabetes or prediabetes, diabetes education and lifestyle support programs can provide additional guidance and encouragement to help manage or prevent the disease.
Step 3: Celebrate Your Progress
Reward yourself whenever you achieve a goal in a way that supports your healthy lifestyle. You might spend time doing your favorite hobby, relaxing, or treating yourself to something meaningful that does not interfere with your health goals.
Remember that healthy habits take time to produce results. If you do not see immediate changes, do not lose motivation. Occasional setbacks are normal, and an important part of success is continuing to move forward instead of giving up.

Healthy Habit Ideas
If you are looking for ways to begin a healthier lifestyle, consider these simple habits:

Eat more fruits and vegetables.
Prepare more meals at home instead of ordering takeout.
Reduce your intake of salty and sugary foods.
Choose whole grains, such as brown rice instead of white rice.
Include short walks in your daily routine.
Try home exercises such as yoga, tai chi, Zumba, or strength training.
Drink more water throughout the day
