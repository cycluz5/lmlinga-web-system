HOW TO PREVENT DENGUE

Explanation:
Dengue fever and dengue hemorrhagic fever are acute infections that can affect infants, children, and adults.

Cause:
Dengue is caused by the bite of an Aedes aegypti mosquito carrying any of the four types of dengue virus.

Mode of Transmission:
The disease is transmitted by the Aedes aegypti mosquito. This mosquito lays eggs in stagnant water and is commonly found in flower pots, cans, rain barrels, tires, and similar containers. It is usually found in dark areas of the house.

Symptoms
Fever lasting 2–7 days
Muscle and joint pain, including back pain
Weakness
Skin rashes
Nose bleeding when the fever subsides
Stomach pain
Vomiting that looks like coffee grounds
Black stools
Difficulty breathing
Treatment
Get enough rest and sleep.
Drink plenty of fluids daily such as water, milk, fruit juice, oral rehydration solution (ORS), barley water, rice water, and light-colored soup.
Wipe the body (except the back) with a damp, lukewarm cloth.
Do not take medicines without a doctor’s prescription.
Consult the nearest health center if the fever lasts for two days or more.





Prevention and Control: Follow the 4S Against Dengue
1. Search and Destroy
Cover water containers and drums.
Change the water in flower pots weekly.
Clean clogged gutters.
Collect and dispose of cans, bottles, and other objects that can hold water.
2. Self-Protection Measures
Wear long pants and long-sleeved clothing.
Use mosquito repellents.
3. Seek Early Consultation
Consult a doctor immediately if fever lasts more than two days or if a rash appears.
4. Support Fogging/Spraying
Support fogging or spraying in hotspot areas when cases increase within two weeks to help prevent an outbreak.

Important Reminder:
Dengue can be deadly, and anyone can get it—children or adults, rich or poor. That is why the whole barangay must work together and cooperate to successfully fight and eliminate dengue.
