THE 10 KUMAINMENTS

10 Healthy Eating Guidelines

1. Eat a Variety of Foods Every Day
Our bodies need different kinds of foods because no single food can provide all the nutrients we need.
There are two types of nutrients:

Macronutrients – These nutrients help the body grow, repair tissues, and provide energy. They include:
Protein: meat, eggs, fish, and milk
Fats: cooking oil, butter, and margarine
Carbohydrates: rice, bread, noodles, and sweet potatoes
Micronutrients – These nutrients help support the body's normal functions and protect against disease. They include vitamins and minerals found in vegetables and fruits such as moringa (malunggay), bananas, water spinach (kangkong), tomatoes, and many others.

2. Exclusively Breastfeed Your Baby for the First Six Months
For the first six months, breast milk is the only food a baby needs. After six months, continue breastfeeding while introducing appropriate complementary foods.
Breast milk:
Contains all the nutrients needed for a baby's growth and brain development.
Helps protect babies from diarrhea, pneumonia, and other illnesses.
Provides protection against diseases such as diabetes and certain types of cancer.

Colostrum, the first milk produced after childbirth, contains antibodies that protect infants from infection. For mothers, breastfeeding helps the uterus return to its normal size more quickly after delivery.



3. Eat Fruits and Vegetables Every Day
Make it a habit to eat fruits and vegetables daily because they provide essential vitamins and minerals that help regulate body functions and strengthen the immune system.
For example:
Vitamin C helps fight infections and promotes faster wound healing.
Fruits are rich in Vitamins A, B, C, K, and many other nutrients.
Eating plenty of fruits and vegetables may help reduce the risk of cancer.
They are also rich in dietary fiber, which promotes healthy digestion and keeps the intestines clean.

4. Eat Fish, Meat, and Other Protein-Rich Foods
Protein is essential for growth and for repairing body tissues. It is especially important for healthy bones and muscles.
Calcium, which strengthens bones and teeth, can be obtained from leafy green vegetables and dairy products.
Good sources of protein include:
Meat
Fish
Chicken
Eggs
Milk
String beans
Mung beans
Crab
Protein also helps build muscles, supports brain development, and contributes to the healthy growth of many parts of the body.

5. Drink Milk and Eat Calcium-Rich Foods
Calcium is essential for building and maintaining strong bones and muscles.
Milk is an excellent source of:
Protein
Vitamins
Minerals
Other good sources of calcium include:
Sardines
Salmon
Moringa (malunggay)
Spinach
Calcium-fortified foods

6. Keep Food and Drinking Water Clean and Safe
Wash your hands before preparing food and before eating.
Many foodborne diseases result from improperly prepared or contaminated food.
Remember to:
Wash fruits, vegetables, meat, and other foods thoroughly before cooking or eating.
Avoid buying food from street vendors if it has been exposed to dust, dirt, insects, and other contaminants.

7. Use Iodized Salt
Using iodized salt helps prevent iodine deficiency disorders and goiter.
A lack of iodine may lead to:
Goiter
Poor metabolism
Learning and intellectual difficulties
Pregnancy complications
Miscarriage
Birth defects such as deafness and inability to speak
Stunted growth

8. Limit Salty, Fatty, and Sugary Foods
Eating too much salty, oily, and sugary food increases the risk of:
High blood pressure
Heart disease
Kidney disease
Diabetes
Obesity
Avoid excessive intake of:
Instant noodles
Canned foods
Salty junk foods
Greasy foods
Sugary foods and beverages
Eating a balanced diet in the right amounts helps prevent many lifestyle-related diseases.

9. Maintain a Healthy Body Weight
Maintaining a healthy weight is important for overall health.
Being overweight or malnourished can both negatively affect the body.
Parents are encouraged to consult their Barangay Nutrition Scholar (BNS) regularly to monitor their children's growth and nutritional status.

10. Stay Physically Active, Avoid Alcohol, and Do Not Smoke
Regular physical activity promotes good health by:
Strengthening the heart
Improving blood circulation
Keeping the lungs healthy
Helping maintain a healthy body weight
Smoking increases the risk of:
High blood pressure
Thickening of the blood
Cancer of the lungs, mouth, throat, and cervix
Blindness
Low birth weight
Birth defects
Excessive alcohol consumption can also negatively affect both physical health and emotional well-being.
Remember
Healthy eating, regular exercise, maintaining a healthy weight, avoiding smoking and excessive alcohol consumption, and practicing good food safety are simple yet effective ways to protect your health and improve your quality of life.
