Cholera

Cause: Cholera is caused by the bacterium Vibrio cholerae.

How It Spreads: Cholera is transmitted through contaminated food and drinking water.

Signs and Symptoms
Sudden onset of watery diarrhea 
Vomiting 
Rapid loss of fluids and electrolytes (dehydration), which may cause: 
Sunken eyes 
Sunken fontanelle (in infants) 
Dry, wrinkled skin 
Immediate Treatment
Replace lost fluids and electrolytes immediately by drinking Oral Rehydration Solution (ORESOL). If ORESOL is unavailable, prepare a homemade solution by mixing: 
1 liter of clean water 
1 teaspoon of salt 
4 teaspoons of sugar 
If diarrhea continues, consult a health worker or bring the patient to the nearest hospital immediately. 
Prevention
Drink only safe and clean drinking water. 
If you are unsure whether the water is safe, boil it for at least 2 minutes before drinking. 
Keep food covered to protect it from insects and rodents. 
Wash and cook food thoroughly. 
Every household should have a clean sanitary toilet. 
Always use a toilet when defecating and keep it clean. 
Wash your hands with soap and water after using the toilet.
