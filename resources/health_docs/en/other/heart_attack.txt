Heart Attack
Heart Attack or Coronary Artery Disease is a disease that is caused by blockage of arteries in the heart from cholesterol build-up. 
It is one of the leading causes of morbidity and mortality worldwide.

What are the symptoms?
Chest pain described as excruciating pain or feeling of chest heaviness that may radiate on the jaw, shoulder and back 
Nausea or vomiting 
Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath 
Cold sweats or dizziness

What are the causes?
Uncontrolled or elevated blood pressure 
Uncontrolled or elevated blood sugar 
High cholesterol or triglyceride levels causing build-up in arteries 
Overweight o obesity

What are the complications?
ABNORMAL HEART RHYTHM 
Irregular heart rhythms that may present as mild as palpitations to lethal complications leading to death
HEART FAILURE 
Decreased in effectivenss of the heart in providing blood to the body. Symptoms may be easy fatigability, difficutly breathing or edema on both legs
SUDDEN CARDIAC ARREST 
If not treated immediately by bringing the patient to the ER, may increase risk for mortality.

How to prevent?
TAKE YOUR MEDICATIONS 
It is important to control your blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and triglyceride to reduce risk for having a heart attack
LIFESTYLE MODIFICATIONS
Balanced diet with less salt, sugar and fat Regular cardiac exercise of 150 minutes o 2.5 hours per week Be mindful of your weight along with balanced diet and exercise Smoking cessation Reduce stressful conditions
