Prevent WILD Diseases This Rainy Season: Influenza (Flu) Illness
What is Influenza (Flu)?
Influenza, or the flu, is a contagious illness caused by the influenza virus that affects the respiratory system. Children, older adults, pregnant women, and people with weakened immune systems are more likely to get this illness.
What are the symptoms of influenza?
High fever 
Runny nose 
Headache 
Snezing 
Cough 
Fatigue 
Sore throat 
Muscle pain 
How can influenza be prevented?
The influenza virus can be spread through:
Direct contact with infected individuals. 
Touching contaminated objects (called fomites), such as toys and doorknobs. 
- The Department of Health (DOH) emphasizes the importance of eating nutritious foods to strengthen the body's immune system. 
- Proper and frequent handwashing with soap and clean water is also recommended to help prevent the spread of infections. 
Proper Ways to Prevent Influenza
Vaccination remains the most effective protection against influenza. 
It is also important to reduce or avoid close contact with people who have influenza. 
Avoid crowded places, keep at least 1 meter away from people who are coughing, and cover your nose and mouth when sneezing or coughing to help prevent the spread of the virus. 
What should you do if you have symptoms of influenza?
Stay at home and avoid direct contact with other people. 
Get enough sleep and drink plenty of water. 
Take paracetamol to reduce fever. (Children should not be given aspirin.) 
Antibiotics should only be used if prescribed to treat complications caused by influenza.
