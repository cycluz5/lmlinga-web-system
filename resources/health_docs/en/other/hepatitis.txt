Hepatitis

- hepatitis means inflammation of the liver. Most often caused by a virus. There are many types, but type B and C are the most common

Symptoms
Dark urine
Light colored stools
Extreme fatigue
Loss of appetite 
Nausea and vomiting
Yellowing skin and eyes

Infection
- Hepatitis B and C are more infectious than HIV and are spread when infected blood or body fluids enters someone’s body

Protecting yourself
Get vaccinated against hepatitis
Do not share needle, toothbrush, razor & nail clipper
Use condom and lubricant when you have sex
Ask for blood products to be screened for hepatitis B & C
Do not smoke and drink alcohol
Wash your hands with soap and water before cooking, eating and after visiting the toilet
