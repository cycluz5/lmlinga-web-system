Seven Healthy Habits for a Healthier Life

The Department of Health (DOH) promotes a Healthy Pilipinas, where making healthy choices becomes easy and accessible for everyone, anytime and anywhere. Adopting these Seven Healthy Habits can improve your overall well-being and help build healthier communities.

1. Move More, Eat Right
Nutrition and Physical Activity
Good nutrition and regular physical activity are the foundation of a long and healthy life. Eating balanced meals and staying physically active help prevent obesity and reduce the risk of chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, cancer, and kidney disease.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
Follow the Pinggang Pinoy healthy plate guide when planning meals.
Engage in at least 30 minutes of physical activity every day.
Read nutrition labels before purchasing packaged foods.
Choose active transportation such as walking, jogging, or cycling whenever possible.
Limit foods high in trans fats, added sugars, and excessive salt.

2. Be Clean, Live Sustainably
Environmental Health
A clean environment and good personal hygiene play an important role in protecting health. Sustainable practices not only benefit individuals but also contribute to healthier communities and a healthier planet.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and clean water for at least 20 seconds.
Practice the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle.
Prepare an emergency go bag for disasters and emergencies.
Use safe and potable water for drinking and food preparation.
Minimize the use of single-use plastics by choosing reusable alternatives.

3. Get Vaccinated
Vaccines and Immunization
Vaccination is one of the most effective ways to protect people of all ages from vaccine-preventable diseases. Staying up to date with recommended vaccines helps strengthen immunity and protects the community.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
Learn about the vaccines recommended for your age and health condition.
Consult your healthcare provider regarding vaccine safety and effectiveness.
Ensure children complete all recommended immunization visits before their first birthday.
Encourage older adults to receive age-appropriate vaccinations.
Educate adolescents and parents about vaccines recommended for young people.

4. Don't Smoke, Limit Alcohol, and Stay Drug-Free
Substance Abuse Prevention
The misuse of tobacco, alcohol, and illegal drugs can seriously affect physical health, mental well-being, relationships, and society. Choosing a substance-free lifestyle promotes better health and improves quality of life.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
Seek professional support if you want to quit smoking.
Learn about the health risks associated with electronic nicotine products and vaping devices.
Avoid the use of illegal drugs.
Participate in rehabilitation programs when necessary.
Drink alcohol responsibly or avoid it altogether.
Know when to seek professional help for substance dependence or addiction.

5. Care for Yourself, Care for Others
Mental Health

Mental health is an essential part of overall health. Taking care of your emotional well-being allows you to manage stress, build healthy relationships, and enjoy a more balanced life.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
Make time for rest, relaxation, and stress management.
Participate in hobbies and activities that bring you joy.
Build and maintain a strong support network of family and friends.
Offer emotional support to others whenever possible.
Monitor your emotions and recognize situations that affect your mental health.
Seek assistance from qualified mental health professionals when needed.

6. Practice Safe Sex
Sexual and Reproductive Health
Sexual and reproductive health is an important aspect of overall well-being. Everyone should have access to accurate information and healthcare services that allow them to make informed decisions while respecting their rights and dignity.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
If sexually active, undergo voluntary testing for HIV and other sexually transmitted infections (STIs).
Learn about modern contraceptive methods and choose those that best meet your needs.
Make informed decisions that support safe pregnancy and motherhood.
Practice responsible and respectful sexual behavior.
Promote healthy, respectful relationships based on consent.
Support gender equality and respect for women and LGBTQ+ individuals.

7. Do No Harm, Put Safety First
Violence and Injury Prevention
Safety should always be a priority at home, in the workplace, and within the community. Preventing injuries and violence helps create a safer environment for everyone.

Healthy Habits to Practice:
Avoid using fireworks and firecrackers; choose safer ways to celebrate special occasions.
Follow water safety guidelines and avoid risky behaviors while swimming.
Wear helmets, seatbelts, and other protective equipment when traveling.
Never drive while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
Report incidents of violence, abuse, or unsafe situations, especially those affecting women, children, and vulnerable groups.
Encourage a culture of safety and responsibility in your family and community.

Live the Seven Healthy Habits Every Day
Living a healthy lifestyle is built on small, consistent choices. By eating nutritious foods, staying physically active, maintaining cleanliness, getting vaccinated, avoiding harmful substances, caring for mental health, practicing safe sexual behaviors, and prioritizing safety, everyone can contribute to a healthier, stronger, and more resilient community.
Together, these Seven Healthy Habits help create a healthier future for all.
