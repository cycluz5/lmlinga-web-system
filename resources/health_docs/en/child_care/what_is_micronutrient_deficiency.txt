WHAT IS MICRONUTRIENT DEFICIENCY?

Does micronutrient deficiency affect our community?

• Yes. It is a lack of important micronutrients such as iron, zinc, and vitamin C, which help support the immune system, growth, and brain development of children.
• Yes, because it is common in our community.

Can a child have micronutrient deficiency even if the child looks healthy and eats well?

• Yes. Micronutrient deficiency is also known as “hidden hunger.” A child may appear full and healthy, but the body may still lack essential nutrients.

• Yes, because many of our usual foods are rice, noodles, bread, meat, and fish. Most of these are rich in macronutrients such as carbohydrates, fats, and proteins, which are important for the body. However, the body cannot fully benefit from them if the child lacks micronutrients. Even if a child eats with a good appetite, eating this way every day may lead to micronutrient deficiency.
