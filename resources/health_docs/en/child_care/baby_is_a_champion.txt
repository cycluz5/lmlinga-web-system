FULLY VACCINATED, PROTECTED CHILD!
WHY ARE VACCINES IMPORTANT?
SAFE AND EFFECTIVE – Protects children against serious diseases such as measles, polio, and pneumonia.
COMMUNITY PROTECTION – Not only is your child protected, but other children in your community are protected as well.
MORE COST-EFFECTIVE – Vaccination costs much less than medical treatment or hospitalization.
A HEALTHIER FUTURE – A child who receives all recommended vaccines is better protected against diseases.
PREVENTS OUTBREAKS – Helps prevent the spread of diseases within the community.
WHAT SHOULD YOU BRING FOR VACCINATION?
Your child's immunization card
Extra diapers and clothes
Milk or water
Medicines, if prescribed
VACCINATION SCHEDULE (DOH EPI)
AT BIRTH: BCG, Hepatitis B
1½, 2½, and 3½ MONTHS: Pentavalent Vaccine, Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV), Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV)
3½ MONTHS AND 9 MONTHS: Inactivated Polio Vaccine (IPV)
9 MONTHS AND 1 YEAR: Measles, Mumps, Rubella Vaccine (MMR)
FOLLOW THE VACCINATION SCHEDULE GIVEN BY YOUR DOCTOR OR HEALTH WORKER.
COMPLETE ALL REQUIRED VACCINES FOR FULL PROTECTION.
COMMON SIDE EFFECTS OF VACCINES
Mild fever
Pain or redness at the injection site
Irritability or drowsiness
CONSULT A HEALTH PROFESSIONAL IMMEDIATELY IF YOUR CHILD HAS:
High fever (above 39°C)
Convulsions (seizures)
Difficulty breathing
GO TO THE NEAREST HEALTH CENTER OR HOSPITAL IMMEDIATELY.
