LEVEL UP: GROWING UP AS A TEEN BOY

WHAT IS PUBERTY?
Puberty is the stage of life when your body, emotions, and mind begin to change as you grow into a teenager. For boys, these changes are mainly caused by the hormone testosterone. All of these changes are completely normal—they simply mean that you are growing up and becoming more mature.

THE STAGES OF PUBERTY
AGE 9
The journey begins!
The testicles start to grow.
Pubic hair begins to appear.

AGE 12–13
The penis continues to grow.Your voice starts to deepen.Body odor becomes stronger.

AGE 13–15
Your muscles become larger and stronger.
Facial hair, including a mustache and beard, begins to grow.
You may start experiencing wet dreams.

AGE 15
Your body becomes more masculine.You may become more interested in the opposite sex.

AGE 16
Rapid physical changes begin to slow down.
However, you may continue growing until early adulthood.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
"IS MASTURBATION NORMAL?"
Yes. Masturbation is a normal part of development and does not have harmful effects.

"WHY DO SOME BOYS MATURE EARLIER THAN OTHERS?"
Everyone develops at their own pace. There is nothing wrong with developing earlier or later than others.

"WHY DO GIRLS USUALLY MATURE EARLIER?"
This is normal. Girls generally begin puberty earlier than boys.

"WHAT DOES ‘FERTILE’ MEAN?"
Once your body begins producing sperm, which may be indicated by wet dreams or ejaculation, you are biologically capable of causing a pregnancy.

WHAT IS HAPPENING ON MY PRIVATE PART?
As you go through puberty, many changes happen "down there"—and THEY ARE ALL COMPLETELY NORMAL!

Pubic Hair - Hair will begin growing around your genitals. At first, it may be thin and straight, but it will gradually become thicker and curlier.

Ejaculation - During an erection, semen containing sperm may be released.

Wet Dreams - Sometimes, while sleeping, you may ejaculate without meaning to. This is called a wet dream, and it is a normal part of puberty.

HYGIENE TIPS FOR BOYS
Take a bath every day using soap.
Use deodorant to help prevent body odor.
Change your underwear and clothes daily.
Wash your genitals regularly and keep them clean.
Take care of your skin to help prevent acne.

CHANGES IN YOUR BODY AND EMOTIONS
Every boy goes through puberty. It is the stage where you gradually grow into a young man. Sometimes these changes may feel stressful or awkward, but that's completely normal—every boy experiences them.

WHAT HAPPEN TO YOUR BODY?
You will continue to grow taller.
A mustache, beard, underarm hair, and pubic hair will begin to grow.
Your voice will deepen, and voice cracks are a normal part of the process.
Your shoulders will become broader, and your body will become stronger.
You may sweat more and develop stronger body odor, so maintaining good hygiene is important.
What Happens to Your Feelings:
You may become moodier or feel irritated more easily.
You may develop romantic feelings or crushes, whether toward girls or boys. These feelings are a normal part of growing up.
You will also begin developing a stronger sense of your own identity and discovering who you are.

TIPS FOR A HEALTHY AND POSITIVE PUBERTY
Don't pressure yourself. Whether your body changes quickly or slowly, your development is normal.
Eat nutritious foods, exercise regularly, and get enough sleep.
Choose friends you can trust.
If you have questions or concerns, talk to your parents, teachers, healthcare providers, or another trusted adult.
Remember that everyone develops at a different pace.
Avoid unhealthy habits such as drinking alcohol, smoking, vaping, or using illegal drugs.
Take good care of both your physical and mental health so you can grow into a healthy and confident adult.

REMEMBER
Everyone experiences puberty differently, and there is no need to feel embarrassed about your body's changes. Puberty is a natural sign that you are growing stronger, becoming more mature, and discovering who you truly are.
